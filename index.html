1

Record the voice memo

~10 minutes · phone voice recorder or Claude voice

This is the whole secret: you already know your launch. It's just trapped in your head as scattered thoughts. Talking pulls it out faster than typing ever will, because you stop editing and start telling the truth.

How to do it: Open your phone's voice recorder. Read each prompt below out loud, then answer it like you're voice-noting a friend who gets your work. Ramble. Backtrack. Say "actually, wait." That mess is the raw ore. You don't need good sentences. You need honest ones.

Your talk track — answer these 8 out loud, in order:

  1. What am I launching, and who is it for?Name the offer. Describe the one person it's built for like they're sitting across from you.
  2. What's the real transformation?Where are they before they buy, where are they after. Skip the features. Talk about the shift.
  3. Why now? Why does this matter to them today?The ache, the timing, the thing that's making this urgent in their life right now.
  4. What do I want to happen, in numbers?Sales, sign-ups, price point, the window you want it to land in. Guess if you have to.
  5. What do I already have?Email list size, audience, content, testimonials, past buyers, anything I can lean on.
  6. What's the offer stack and the price?What they get, payment options, and the price. Two things to name out loud even if you're unsure: a bonus you could add, and a limited-time offer (a deadline, cart close, or price increase). These two lift conversions the most, so don't skip them.
  7. What's stopping them from buying?Every objection, doubt, and "but what about..." you can think of. Say them all.
  8. What feels scary or unclear about this launch?Be honest. This tells the plan where you need the most support.

Don't have 10 minutes to record? Open Claude on your phone, tap the voice button, and just talk through the 8 prompts live. It transcribes as you go — skip straight to Move 2.

Hand it to the forge

Get your words into text, then run one prompt
First, get a transcript. Three easy ways: (1) record inside Claude's voice mode and it transcribes automatically, (2) use your phone's built-in voice-to-text while you talk, or (3) play the memo back and let your Notes app dictate it. Don't clean it up. Typos and "ums" are fine — Claude reads right through them.

Then open a new chat with Claude, paste the master prompt below, and paste your transcript where it says to. Hit enter. That's the forge.

⚒ The Master Prompt 
You are my launch strategist. You think like a grounded, fiery direct-response marketer who also respects that I run a soul-led business. No corporate fluff, no hype, no "crush it" energy.

Below is a raw, rambly voice-memo transcript where I brain-dumped about a launch. It's messy and out of order on purpose.

Your job: turn it into a clear, structured launch plan I can actually run. Read the whole thing first, then build the plan in these sections:

1. LAUNCH AT A GLANCE — the offer, who it's for, the price, the dates/window, and my goal in one tight paragraph.
2. THE CORE PROMISE — the one-sentence transformation, plus 3 ways to say it (for an email subject, a post hook, and a sales-page headline).
3. AUDIENCE & TIMING — who this is for and why now matters to them today.
4. THE OFFER STACK — what they get, bonuses, price, payment options, and the deadline/scarcity.
   Then check for the two biggest conversion levers and FLAG anything missing in a clearly labeled callout called "⚑ MISSING LAUNCH LEVERS":
   - If I did NOT include a bonus, flag it and suggest 1-2 specific bonuses that would fit this offer.
   - If I did NOT include a limited-time offer (a real deadline, cart close, price increase, or bonus expiry), flag it and suggest a concrete one.
   - If I'm missing BOTH, say so plainly and tell me these are the two highest-leverage things to add before I launch. Do not let me skip past this.
5. OBJECTIONS & ANSWERS — every objection I named (and any I missed), each with a one-line reframe I can use in content.
6. THE LAUNCH RUNWAY — a day-by-day or phase-by-phase plan from today to cart close. For each phase: what to post/send, the angle, and the goal of that touch.
7. CONTENT STARTERS — 5 specific post or email ideas with hooks, mapped to the runway.
8. WHERE YOU NEED SUPPORT — based on what I said felt scary or unclear, flag the 2-3 riskiest parts and tell me exactly what to do about each.

Rules:
- Use ONLY what's in my transcript. If something critical is missing (like a price or a date), don't invent it — list it under "DECISIONS YOU STILL NEED TO MAKE" at the very end.
- Match my voice: grounded, real, a little fiery. Never use em dashes. Never tell me I'm broken.
- Be specific and usable. No generic marketing advice.

When you're done, ask me ONE sharp question that would most improve the plan.

Run it and sharpen it

What comes out, and how to make it sharper

In about 30 seconds you'll have a full launch plan. Here's the structure it gives you back:

Now sharpen it. The first draft is a strong skeleton. These follow-up prompts turn it into something you'd actually publish. Copy any one and send it in the same chat:

↳ Refine: write the content 
Take Content Starter #1 and write it out in full, in my voice. Then give me 2 alternate hooks for it.
↳ Refine: build the runway out 
Turn the Launch Runway into a checklist with exact dates, starting from today. For each day, list the one most important action. Keep it realistic for a solo founder.
↳ Refine: pressure-test it 
Be honest with me. What's the weakest part of this launch plan, and what would you change to make it convert better? Don't be polite, be useful.
↳ Build: turn it into a Launch Plan artifact 
Now build me a single self-contained HTML artifact of this entire launch plan that I can open in my browser and actually run my launch from. Make it one .html file, no external dependencies except Google Fonts.

Include:
- A header with the offer name, my core promise, the price, and the launch window/cart-close date.
- A countdown or clear "days until cart close" at the top.
- An interactive CHECKLIST for the full Launch Runway — every action as a checkbox that I can tick off, grouped by phase or day. Save my progress in localStorage so checked items stay checked when I reopen it.
- A progress bar showing how much of the runway I've completed.
- A collapsible section for each part of the plan: Core Promise (all 3 versions), Offer Stack, Objections & Answers, and the Content Starters with their hooks.
- A "copy" button on each content starter so I can grab the text fast.

Design it dark and premium: near-black background, warm copper and ember accents, a serif display font (Cormorant Garamond) for headings and a clean sans (Hanken Grotesk) for body. Grounded and a little fiery, never corporate. No em dashes anywhere in the copy.

Use only the real details from my launch plan above. If a date or price is still undecided, put a clear "[ DECIDE THIS ]" placeholder instead of inventing it. Give me the complete file.
